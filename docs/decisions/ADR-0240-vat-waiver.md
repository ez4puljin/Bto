## Context

A wholesale customer either takes the VAT receipt of a sale or, in its place, has the debt reduced. Until now the accountant wrote that reduction as a manual journal entry and paired it with the invoice by hand; nothing on the invoice said that its VAT receipt would not be issued, and nobody could list a customer's sales by whether the receipt is owed.

## Decision

1. **The amount.** A waiver takes the percent of the invoice's net amount: its total, VAT included, less the totals of its credit notes, times the percent, two decimals HALF_UP (`vatWaiverAmount` in `@tugeel/shared/finance`, through `@tugeel/shared/money`). The percent (default 2.00, CHECK `0 < pct ≤ 100`) and the entry's words (default «Борлуулалтын хөнгөлөлт») are finance settings (`finance_settings.vat_waiver_pct`, `vat_waiver_description`; null = the defaults). Both are frozen on the waiver's row with the base amount, so a later change of the settings reaches no waiver already made.
2. **Refusals.** An invoice is not waived when it is VOID, when it carries a live waiver, when its returns left nothing (or the discount rounds to nothing), or when a company (B2B) e-Barimt receipt for it is alive — `ebarimt_receipts.receipt_type = 'B2B'` in PENDING, SENDING, CONFIRMED or INDETERMINATE: the customer already took, or may have taken, the VAT receipt. A consumer (B2C) receipt, a receipt in ERROR or CANCELLED, or none does not refuse. After a waiver, finance's own path to a receipt (`InvoiceEbarimtService.retry`, `POST /invoices/:id/ebarimt`) refuses to prepare a B2B receipt for that invoice until the waiver is taken back; a B2C one is prepared as before. The ebarimt module is not changed and knows nothing of waivers.
3. **The books stay as they are.** The invoice's own entry, its VAT line and the VAT report are untouched: the waiver is only the discount entry. **TODO-VERIFY (accountant):** whether the VAT of a sale whose receipt was waived is to be treated differently (declared, reduced, reported apart) is the owner's and the accountant's later decision; nothing is built for it. Until then the VAT report shows such a sale as any other.
4. **The entry.** One Finance Core document per waiver through the intake (ADR-0071): source `BTO`, source doc type `VAT_WAIVER`, source doc id the waiver's own id, document type **`BTO.VAT_WAIVER`** (class `AR_CREDIT`, numbered by the core's series `VAT_WAIVER`, «НХ-{year}{month}{day}-» with four digits until the accountant chooses another; the boot step and migration 0123 write the series and the type). Two lines: Dr the counter account by a posting key — the customer's own account by its key `VAT_WAIVER_<code>`, else the sales discount role's key `SALES_DISCOUNT` (520201) — and Cr AR on the customer's party with `settles` on the invoice (`BTO / SALES_INVOICE / <invoice id>`) for `min(amount, the invoice's open amount)`. What an invoice already paid cannot take stays the customer's open credit, exactly as a credit note's rest. No VAT or city-tax line. The entry is dated today's business date (Asia/Ulaanbaatar); a closed month refuses as the core does (`FIN_PERIOD_CLOSED`). The entry's description is the frozen words behind the document's number, as for every core-numbered document. `invoices.open_amount` follows the item through `FinPosting.syncInvoices`, never written here. The POST carries an `Idempotency-Key`; the lock order is a credit note's: the customer, then the invoice FOR UPDATE.
5. **The undo.** «Буцаах» is the core's REVERSE of that document: its allocation is undone, the mirror entry is posted today, the invoice's open amount comes back. The waiver's row stays with status REVERSED, who, when and an optional reason. The invoice then takes VAT again and may be waived again (a new row, a new document). The core refuses the reversal while another allocation sits on the waiver's own credit (`FIN_REVERSAL_BLOCKED`): that allocation is reversed first.
6. **Data** (migration `0123_vat_waiver`, additive): `vat_waivers` (invoice, customer, status POSTED / REVERSED, the entry's date, percent, base amount, amount, account id and code, description, the core's document id and number, the entry's id, created by / at, reversed by / at / reason, the reversal's entry id; at most one POSTED row per invoice by the partial unique index `vat_waivers_invoice_posted`; `REVOKE DELETE, TRUNCATE` from `app`); `customer_counter_accounts` (customer id → account id; a setting, removed when the customer goes back to the default). The customer's account is finance's own table keyed by the customer: `customers` is not changed and partners does not come to depend on finance.
7. **The counter account a customer may carry** is ACTIVE, carries no control (not a receivable, a payable or an advance: such a line needs a party), is not a money account (control MONEY or the journal CASH / BANK / MONEY) and is not stock (the journal INVENTORY or the role INVENTORY). It is checked when it is saved, by a holder of `finance.settings.manage`, who also writes the account's posting key and rule (a waiver is posted as source BTO by someone who may hold no settings right, and the intake takes an account code only from the core's own source); and again when a waiver is posted (422 when the account was switched off or changed kind since).
8. **The right.** A new permission `finance.vat_waiver.manage`, «НӨАТ хасуулах», guards the waiver and its undo. ADMIN holds it as it holds everything; no other system role holds it by default (the owner: only a specially permitted person); it is not ADMIN-only, so the owner ticks it on a role in «Систем → Дүр» (ADR-0165). It is Bto's own right: the Finance Core's contract does not list it, and the posting goes through the intake as the source. Reading the list and the totals needs `finance.read`; the customer's account and the two settings `finance.settings.manage`.

## The surface

- `POST /invoices/:id/vat-waiver`, `POST /invoices/:id/vat-waiver/reverse` (`{reason?}`) [`finance.vat_waiver.manage`, idempotent]: the invoice as its panel reads it.
- `GET /invoices` takes `vatWaived=true|false`; every invoice carries `vatWaiver` (the live waiver or null), `vatWaiverPreview` (`pct`, `baseAmount`, `amount`, `blockedBy` VOID / VAT_WAIVED / NOTHING_LEFT / B2B_RECEIPT or null) and `receipt` (the e-Barimt receipt's type and status, or null).
- `GET /customer-balances/:customerId/vat-totals?from&to` [`finance.read`]: the customer's issued invoices of the range split into «НӨАТ авах» and «НӨАТ авахгүй» (count, total, VAT, open amount, the waivers' discounts).
- `GET` / `PUT /customer-balances/:customerId/counter-account` [`finance.read` / `finance.settings.manage`]; `GET` / `PUT /finance-settings/vat-waiver` [the same].
- Refusals use the standing codes with a stable `params.status`: 409 `INVOICE_STATE_INVALID` with `VOID`, `VAT_WAIVED`, `NOTHING_LEFT`, `B2B_RECEIPT` (a waiver) or `NOT_VAT_WAIVED` (an undo); 409 `EBARIMT_RECEIPT_STATE_INVALID` with `VAT_WAIVED` (a B2B receipt after a waiver); 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` on `counterAccount` (`active`, `control`, `money`, `inventory`, `usable`) and on `pct` / `description`. The web says each in the owner's words.
- The web: `/finance/customers/:id` gains «Харилцсан данс» under the balance and two views, «Тооцооны дэвтэр» (unchanged, the default) and «Борлуулалт» (`?tab=sales`, `?vat=takes|waived`); «Санхүү → Тохиргоо» the card «НӨАТ хасуулах»; the invoice panel the fact «НӨАТ авахгүй».

## Known limits

- **A credit note made after a waiver does not recompute the waiver.** The waiver keeps the base and the amount it froze. To book the right amount the waiver is taken back, the return is made and the invoice is waived again; the guide says so.
- The customer's ledger shows a waiver as «Залруулга» (`ADJUSTMENT`) with the document's number, and the journal as a manual-source entry: a kind of its own would change the customer app's statement and is left for a later task. The customer app's invoice shows the open amount after the waiver, but counts the discount neither as paid nor as credited.
- The invoices' list reads each invoice's e-Barimt receipt one at a time through the ebarimt module (it has no read of many); a page is 50 invoices.
- «Ашгийн самбар» (ADR-0238) does not read `BTO.VAT_WAIVER` documents yet; a later task puts them on the board. The document type string is stable for it.
- Dedicated error codes (in place of `params.status`) would need the shared error catalog and its status map, outside this task.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A credit note without goods.** It would post sales returns and reverse VAT, change the invoice's returned quantities and mix a commercial discount into the returns' reports.
- **A manual journal entry and an allocation** (today's way): two steps by the accountant, nothing on the invoice, no list.
- **An account code on the intake line.** The intake refuses `accountCode` from a source other than the core's own screens (ADR-0071); a key per account keeps the rule (ADR-0154, ADR-0176).
- **A column on `customers` for the counter account.** Partners would carry a finance fact and depend on the chart.
