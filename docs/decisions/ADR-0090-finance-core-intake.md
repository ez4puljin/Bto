# ADR-0090: The Finance Core's intake and open items (F2): one engine for every document, Bto's receivables on it, Finance I's receivables dropped

| Implements | ADR-0083 #2 and #3 (phase F2), ADR-0071 (the intake contract in-process), ADR-0072 #1, #2, #5–#9, #11 (classes, controls, open items, allocations, party balances, the manual allocation, reversals), ADR-0073 #4 and #5 (exposure and the default overdue rule), within ADR-0088 (one company per installation) |
| Amends | ADR-0073 #3 (the invoice's open amount is a mirror written in the posting's transaction, not a read from the core, until H1), ADR-0089 #4 and #6 (the journal numbers are the core's; the façade keeps only the POS entries and refuses party-control lines), ADR-0060 #5 (a refund opens a debit item the accountant pairs by hand), ADR-0071's v1.0 limits (an attribute value may hold 300 characters) |

## Context

F1 (ADR-0084, ADR-0089) built the organisation and the ledger: the chart, money accounts, append-only rules, monthly periods and the journal in schema `fin`, with Bto posting through the façade `FinJournalPosting`. Finance I still kept its own receivables: `ar_entries`, `customer_balances`, `payments` and `allocations`, and the credit check read them.

F2 (ADR-0083 §16.0) builds the engine those tables become: the intake contract `fin.intake/1` (ADR-0071), documents, open items, allocations and party balances (ADR-0072), the exposure the credit check reads (ADR-0073), the accountant's manual allocation and the core's reconcile. Each table it replaces is dropped in the same phase, with no copy (ADR-0083 #2).

On 2026-10-20 the development database held two POS shift entries and no invoice, voucher, credit note or manual entry.

## Decision

1. **One intake, in-process.** `FinIntakeInTx` (`finance-core/posting/intake.service.ts`) takes every command: `submitInTx(tx, ctx, cmd)` inside the source's transaction (a sale, a voucher, a return), `submit(ctx, cmd)` in its own (the core's screens; a refusal is then kept REJECTED, or HELD for a closed month, with an attempt row written by a second short transaction). The steps and the lock order are ADR-0071 #6: parse and hash (decimals at scale 2, revision 1, `accountingDate = documentDate`; `commandId`, actor and contract minor not hashed), authorise, claim the key (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, then `FOR UPDATE`), the class rules and the period, the parties (`party_companies FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, id order), the money accounts (`FOR SHARE`), the rules and the controls, the settled items (`FOR UPDATE`), the numbers last, then the writes. POSTED and DUPLICATE answer; every other outcome throws `FinError` and the caller rolls back (D11).
2. **The classes built.** AR_INVOICE and AP_BILL (one receivable or payable line, or none when nothing is owed; never settles), AR_CREDIT and AP_CREDIT (one line on the control account, settling named targets), RECEIPT and PAYMENT (exactly one MONEY line with its money account; party lines settle FIFO or named targets; the rest stays open), JOURNAL (balanced; only the core's own source FIN names `accountCode`), ALLOCATION (no lines, no entry; pairs existing items of the header party on the key's account) and REVERSE. SETTLEMENT and OPENING have no F2 caller and refuse (`FIN_CLASS_RULE CLASS_NOT_BUILT`) until F5's opening import and D6.
3. **The controls are enforced.** A line on a RECEIVABLE, PAYABLE or ADVANCE account needs its party and opens exactly one item (a deferred trigger refuses a control line without one at commit); a line on a MONEY account needs its money account, which must book to that account; a key with `requires_tax_code` needs its `tax` (code and base; verifying the amount against a rate waits for the tax tables of F6). A missing rule rejects (`FIN_MAPPING_MISSING`); nothing goes to a suspense account. Owner, 2026-10-20: the manual entry dialog asks for the cash box or bank account of a line on a cash or bank account, and for the customer of a line on the receivables.
4. **Numbers.** The core numbers from its series (`fin.number_series`, gap-free `fin.number_counters` per rendered prefix, taken last): PAYMENT (`Т-`, vouchers), JOURNAL (`Ж-`, every journal entry, the POS entries of the façade included) and ALLOCATION (`Х-{year}{month}{day}-`, TODO-VERIFY with the accountant). Bto keeps `Н-` and `К-` (document types in SOURCE mode keep the sender's number). Migration 0034 moves the `JE_NO` and `PAY_NO` counters into the core, so the day's next number follows the last one. Bto's «Дугаарлалт» page edits the core's series for vouchers and entries. A typed journal entry's document number is its entry's number.
5. **The invoice's open amount is a mirror** (amends ADR-0073 #3 in mechanism). `invoices.open_amount` stays until H1, rewritten in the same transaction from the invoice's item after every posting that touches it (`FinPosting.syncInvoices`, from the result's items and allocations through `FinReader.itemsInTx`), so the invoice lists, their filters, the aging and the home tile keep their SQL. The reconcile compares it with the item and rewrites it on a drift.
6. **Bto's documents in the core.** The invoice is `BTO/SALES_INVOICE/<id>` (`BTO.SALES_INVOICE`), the credit note `BTO/SALES_RETURN/<id>` (`BTO.SALES_RETURN`, settling its invoice for `min(total, open)` as today), a voucher `FIN/RECEIPT|PAYMENT/<Idempotency-Key>` (`FIN.CUSTOMER_RECEIPT`, `FIN.CUSTOMER_REFUND`, `FIN.OTHER_RECEIPT`, `FIN.OTHER_PAYMENT`, posted as the user), a manual entry `FIN/JOURNAL/<Idempotency-Key>` (`FIN.JOURNAL`, posting key `MANUAL`), an allocation `FIN/ALLOCATION/<Idempotency-Key>` (`FIN.ALLOCATION`). Bto keeps choosing what a payment settles (oldest due first or the accountant's choice, its invoices locked first) and names the targets; the core books and checks them. A refund settles nothing on its own: its debit item waits for the accountant's allocation (D7). A voucher's own description, reference, counterparty and who handed it in are the document's attributes (`memo`, `reference`, `counterparty`, `collectedBy`); the voucher's id is the core's document id (the POS hand-over and the payment notifications keep it as a plain id).
7. **Credit.** `CreditControlService` reads `FinReader.exposureInTx` after the customer lock (the party locked in the same transaction): HOLD; OVERDUE when the overdue amount exceeds the open credits (the default of ADR-0073 #5; the new setting `credit.overdue_nets_credits`, on by default, returns to "any overdue amount" when off); LIMIT on the net receivable plus the open orders. `CREDIT_BLOCKED` gains `creditsOpen`.
8. **The manual allocation.** `GET /customer-balances/:customerId/open-items`, `POST /finance/allocations` and `POST /finance/allocations/:id/reverse` (`finance.payments.record`, `@Idempotent`); the dialog «Тулгалт хийх» on the customer's ledger page; an invoice's allocations show the ALLOCATION documents with «Тулгалтыг буцаах».
9. **The façade narrows.** `FinJournalPosting` keeps only what pos posts until H2 (the shift close): its number comes from the core's series, it refuses a party-control line (`FIN_CLASS_RULE CONTROL_LINE_NEEDS_INTAKE`) and applies the money control.
10. **Reconcile.** `reconcileBooks` records three `fin.reconcile_runs` per company: CACHE (party balances against items, rewritten), ITEMS (each open amount against its amount less its allocations) and SUBLEDGER_GL (items against the control lines). `pnpm finance:reconcile` and the nightly job run it and Bto's mirror check (invoices against items) into `finance_reconcile_runs`; any drift exits 2.
11. **No copy; what stops the migration.** Migration 0034 drops `ar_entries`, `customer_balances`, `payments` and `allocations`, the FKs `pos_shifts.handover_payment_id` and `customer_notifications.payment_id` into `payments`, and the voucher and journal formats of `finance_settings`. A database that holds a voucher, or an F1 journal entry of an invoice, credit note or voucher, stops it (nothing could post them again; the F1 precedent, ADR-0089 #8). The development re-post (`pnpm finance:repost-journal`) posts Bto's invoices, credit notes and invoice voids again through the intake. The walk copies `tugeel_ca3` and `tugeel_rep` stay at their migrations (owner, 2026-10-20) and are made again from `tugeel` when needed.
12. **Contract.** `V1_LIMITS.maxAttributeValue` is loosened from 200 to 300 characters (allowed within 1.x), so a voucher's own description fits an attribute. The in-process result also carries the entry's id (`document.journalEntryId`), which Bto stores beside its documents.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Read the invoice's open amount from the core in every list | The lists filter and sort on it in SQL, and Bto may not name `fin` tables; a mirror written in the same transaction and reconciled nightly gives the same figures with no cross-schema query, until H1 moves the lists. |
| Keep a Bto `vouchers` table beside the core's documents | Two records of one voucher; the core's RECEIPT / PAYMENT documents carry everything the screens show. |
| Let a refund settle the open credits FIFO | It would change what today's screens show (a refund's `allocatedAmount`, a payment's settlements) and apply credits without the accountant (D7). |
| Copy Finance I's vouchers into the core | ADR-0083 #2: no copy; the development database holds none. |
| Allow a manual line on a cash account without its cash box | The owner chose the picker (2026-10-20); the money control keeps the cash book and the journal in step. |

## Consequences

- Every receivable, voucher, manual entry and allocation is a core document with its intake key; a resend is a DUPLICATE, another payload under a key a CONFLICT.
- The customer's balance, ledger and statement read the core (the ledger now also shows a manual entry on the receivables as ADJUSTMENT); the open invoices, aging and the home tile read the mirror.
- The money accounts' balances and the cash book read the core's live vouchers.
- `FinError` codes reach the web (problem+json): `FIN_OVER_ALLOCATION`, `FIN_REVERSAL_BLOCKED` (a voucher whose remainder an allocation uses is voided only after that allocation is reversed), `FIN_PARTY_REQUIRED`, `FIN_MONEY_ACCOUNT_REQUIRED`.
- Open: the allocation's number format and the city-tax code per activity (`MN.CITY.STD` for now) wait for the accountant (TODO-VERIFY); a refund's open debit item counts as overdue after its date until the accountant pairs it (the default overdue rule nets it against the credit it refunded).
