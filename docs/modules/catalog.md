# Module `catalog` — contract

Status: **built** in delivery 1 (week 2), session of 2026-10-11; accepted locally together with the rest of delivery 1. On 2026-10-13 (ADR-0044): brands removed, category code ranges, products waiting for their prices, the e-Barimt card, and product registration in one step (run by inventory). On 2026-10-14 (ADR-0049, ADR-0050, ADR-0051): the list's filters on other modules' data, its count, ticked rows, Excel, bulk status and category changes and per-user columns. Code: `apps/api/src/modules/catalog/`, shared rules in `packages/shared/src/{text,gtin,units,files}`, screens in `apps/web/src/features/catalog/`, scanning in `apps/web/src/lib/scan/` and `apps/web/src/components/{scan-box,camera-scanner}.tsx`. Decisions: ADR-0008 (units and factors), ADR-0010 (money and tax), ADR-0011 (i18n), ADR-0015 #3 (e-Barimt attributes optional in delivery 1), ADR-0028 (search keys), ADR-0029 (barcode model), ADR-0030 (file storage), ADR-0031 (camera fallback), ADR-0044 (registration, code ranges, sale readiness, no brands), architecture §4 (catalog row) and §5.2.

catalog depends only on `identity` (audit, settings, storage, access decorators). partners (`product_suppliers`, ADR-0032), inventory, imports, pricing, sales, procurement and ebarimt import its `index.ts`.

## 1. Owner's answers behind the design (interview of 2026-10-10/11)

Names mix Cyrillic and Latin; weighed goods are sold in bulk by the kilogram; pre-packed bundles are ordinary products and promotional bundles belong to pricing (delivery 2); pieces, blocks and boxes carry their own barcodes and some products have none; the product code (SKU) is numeric and comes from the owner's Excel sheet, the system suggests the next one; categories form a tree; only the admin edits the catalog; VAT-exempt goods exist and city tax is undecided until the accountant answers (SPEC §11 #3a); the admin uploads photos by hand. The Excel columns map as follows: code, name, category, barcode, box quantity and unit weight → catalog; home warehouse → inventory (week 3, `product_locations`); price → the delivery-2 price import; supplier → partners `product_suppliers` (ADR-0032); image → uploaded by hand.

On 2026-10-12, checking the screens, the owner asked for every piece of product information as a column of the product list, chosen with a «Баганууд» button and remembered on the computer, without stock columns; and could not find how to edit a product from the list (ADR-0037).

On 2026-10-13 (ADR-0044) the owner changed how products are registered: the category first, which suggests the next code from its own range (a subcategory without one uses its parent's; a typed code outside the range is refused, imports excepted); no brands; no e-Barimt data at registration (the accountant enters it on its own card); the supplier, at least one photo, the home warehouse and the box quantity are required, the shelf optional; saving registers the product, which then waits until a price clerk enters its prices and completes it (since 2026-10-14 the warehouse price, the price profile and the pack counted whole, ADR-0048). Waiting products may be used in the warehouse; imported new products wait too.

## 5. Rules worth knowing

- **Search (ADR-0028).** Keys are built in `@tugeel/shared/text` from the name and the SKU: `search_text` (NFKC, lower case, Latin look-alikes → Cyrillic, ё → е) and `search_phonetic` (romanised, ө → o, ү → u, Latin spelling folded). Every term must match; terms shorter than 3 characters match word starts only; `%` and `_` are escaped. After changing the rules, raise `SEARCH_KEYS_VERSION` and run `pnpm catalog:reindex-search` (`--all` recomputes every product).
- **Barcodes (ADR-0029, ADR-0098 #6).** Input is cleaned and classified by `@tugeel/shared/gtin`: GTIN-8/12/13/14 with a valid check digit share one key (UPC-A "036000291452" = EAN-13 "0036000291452"), UPC-E expands to UPC-A; other printable ASCII codes (Code 128 labels, and since 2026-10-22 digits of GTIN length with a wrong check digit, which the old system holds) are stored as typed and found by exactly that code. The phone camera still ignores a GTIN-length read with a wrong check digit as a misread (`isMisreadGtin`). The web checks the same rules before sending.
- **Units (ADR-0008).** Factors never change. A piece-based product needs whole factors and whole base quantities; a kilogram-based product allows 3 decimals. A product needs a new unit row (e.g. "хайрцаг 12") for a second box size, because (product, unit of measure) is unique among current units.
- **Images (ADR-0030).** The browser scales each photo to 1280 px and a 256 px thumbnail (JPEG, EXIF removed), uploads both through presigned URLs, then attaches them; HEIC is refused with a hint on desktop browsers.
- **Code ranges (ADR-0044 #1).** The rules live in `@tugeel/shared/catalog` (`checkSku`, `rangeOwner`, `rangeConflict`, `nextAfter`, `lowestFree`) for the api and the web. A code belongs to the innermost range around it; a category uses its own range, else its nearest ancestor's. Imports never ask; moving a product keeps its code. A two-key advisory lock on the number (`SKU_NUMBER_LOCK`) serialises typed codes with the same number.
- **Waiting for prices (ADR-0044 #2).** A new product has `sale_ready_at` NULL until pricing completes it; `status` is unchanged, so the warehouse uses waiting products as any other. Orders take only complete ones (SPEC §6).
- **Scanning (ADR-0021, ADR-0031).** Barcode fields and the global wedge detector read number-row digits from `event.code` (the Mongolian layout types symbols there). The camera opens on HTTPS or localhost only.

## 7. Audit

Actions `CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `SUPERSEDE`, `RETIRE`, `ASSIGN`, `COMPLETE` (a product completed by pricing) on the entities `uoms`, `categories`, `products`, `product_units`, `product_barcodes`, `product_images` (labels in `mn.json` `audit.*`; `brands` stays for rows written before 2026-10-13). ## 8. Screens (`apps/web`)

Menu group «Бараа» (layout and patterns: `docs/ui.md`, ADR-0043):

- `/catalog/products`: search and filters in the URL, read through `POST /products/query` with the count in the header («1,751 бараа»). Besides the category and status pills, «Шүүлтүүр» (ADR-0049) opens one dialog per field: segment, supplier (any link or the main one), home warehouse, price profile, warehouse price range, stock (in, out, at or below the minimum; one warehouse or every selling one), created and changed dates (today, 7 or 30 days, a range) and missing data (no base-unit barcode, no barcode, no photo, no weight, e-Barimt data, no supplier, no home, no prices; any of those chosen). Active filters show as chips (a click reopens the dialog, its button removes the filter; «Бүгдийг арилгах»), kept in the URL in short form (`supplier=main:1,2`, `price=1000~5000`, `stock=out:3`, `created=30d`, `missing=photo,weight`, `segment=7`; `features/catalog/product-filters.ts`) and sent as one definition (the fields ANDed, the missing data ORed). «Сегмент болгох» (`segments.manage`) opens the segment editor with them. Whoever may export, change products in bulk or manage segments ticks rows: a label-wrapped checkbox column (a click beside the box never opens the product), «Бүгдийг сонгох» over the loaded rows. The ticks stay while the search and the filters change, so the ticks of several searches go together, and live in the tab's `sessionStorage` per user (`tugeel.catalog.products.selection.v1:<userId>`, `features/catalog/use-product-selection.ts`; a product opened and «back» or a reload keeps them, another user starts with none; ADR-0052). When every loaded row is ticked and the filters keep more, the bar offers «Таарсан бүгдийг сонгох (N)» (`POST /products/query/ids`), then says «Таарсан бүх N бараа сонгогдсон»; unticking the header then unticks every match; past 20,000 it only says why. The selection bar (`components/selection-bar.tsx`) floats at the list's foot: the count, «Excel татах», «Засах» (category, main supplier, price profile from a day, home warehouse, then «Идэвхгүй болгох» and «Идэвхжүүлэх» — each with its right, `features/catalog/bulk-actions.tsx`), the «Сегмент» menu (`segments.manage`, `docs/modules/segments.md` §8), then «Сонголтыг цуцлах». The changes act on every ticked product, loaded or not; above 500 a dialog only explains the limit; a dialog names the count and its rule, the home move runs a dry run and names the products with stock elsewhere first (reading the names of ticks the list has not loaded), a toast says what changed, what already was so and what had no terms. Without ticks «Excel татах» (`catalog.products.export`) writes every row the filters keep. Every piece of product information is a column (image, SKU, name, category, base unit, other units «1 хайрцаг = 24 ш», base and other-unit barcodes, the warehouse price and the price profile from pricing (ADR-0048), weight, home warehouse and fixed shelves from inventory, main supplier «+N» from partners, status; the tax data is on the e-Barimt card, not in the list), read per page with `productIds` only for visible columns. «Баганууд» hides and shows columns (SKU and name stay), remembered per user in this browser (`localStorage` `tugeel.catalog.products.hiddenColumns.v1:<userId>`, starting from the browser's old shared key; ADR-0050); the shared `components/column-chooser.tsx` serves the customer and supplier lists too (ADR-0037). A click anywhere on a row opens the product, whose name stays a link (ADR-0042 #6). The status pill filters complete, «Үнэ хүлээгдэж буй» and inactive products, and waiting rows carry that tag. «Шинэ бараа» shows with `catalog.products.register`. The page uses the full width (route `handle: { wide: true }`).
