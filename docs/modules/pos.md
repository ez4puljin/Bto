The supermarket's eight tills talk to a **store edge server** on the store's LAN (S2); the edge replays every document to the centre, which is the system of record for stock and money. Sections 1–8 are the centre's half of the contract; §9 is the edge's.

## 4. Rules worth knowing

- **The till's warehouse (ADR-0123).** Stock leaves and returns onto the *till's* STORE warehouse (SALESFLOOR / DAMAGED), the POS_SHORTFALL adjustment and the credit sale's invoice name it, the shift's entry carries it; the edge's warehouse is only the default for its tills and the snapshot's `node`. One Edge per supermarket, any number of sales floors with their own stock.
- **Accepting a sale (ADR-0063 #4, #5).** In order: the duplicate check on `(edge, client_id)`; the till by code (active); the shift by `client_id`, locked FOR UPDATE, OPEN, of that till; the cashier active; a return's supervisor when the setting asks; the products and units (a unit belongs to its product, the frozen factor is the unit's); the money (`subtotal = qty × unit price`, `total = subtotal − discount`, `vat = taxSplit(total, rate, 0)`, the VAT rate against `TaxService.companyRates(sold day)` + `ratesFor(product)`, the header as the sum of the lines, the tenders summing to the total); for a return its sale (locked), the window, the sold lines (same product and unit, same unit price, no more than what is left). Then the rows, then stock: the SALESFLOOR balances of the sold products are locked (`StockQueryService.lockOnHandInBin`), what is missing is adjusted in by `AdjustmentsService.postSystemInTx` with `POS_SHORTFALL` (one line per product, the base unit, `pos_shortfalls` rows) and the sale posts `POS_SALE` (external document `POS`) in full; a return posts `RETURN_IN` onto SALESFLOOR, or DAMAGED for damaged lines, at the warehouse's average cost. The effective date is the posting time.
- **Refusals.** An `AppError` other than `BIN_LOCKED_FOR_COUNT` / `STOCK_INSUFFICIENT` is permanent: the document is logged in `pos_sync_rejections` and the batch continues; those two are thrown so the edge resends the batch (applied documents answer DUPLICATE then). Codes: `POS_TOTALS_MISMATCH` (422, `field`, `lineNo`, `expected`, `got`), `POS_TAX_MISMATCH` (422), `POS_SHIFT_STATE_INVALID` (409, `status`), `POS_TILL_BUSY` (409), `POS_TILL_INACTIVE` (409), `POS_CASHIER_INACTIVE` (409), `POS_RETURN_EXCEEDS` (422, `remaining`), `POS_RETURN_WINDOW` (422, `windowDays`), `POS_NUMBER_TAKEN` (409), `POS_NOT_CONFIGURED` (409), `POS_DEV_ONLY` (404).
- **The close (ADR-0064 #2).** Totals come from the shift's accepted documents, never from the till; `expected = float + cash sales − cash refunds`, `variance = counted − expected`; the entry Dr `POS_CASH` (counted − float), Dr `CARD_CLEARING` (card net), Dr `SALES_RETURN` + Dr `VAT_PAYABLE` (returns), Cr `POS_SALES` + Cr `VAT_PAYABLE` (sales), the variance on `CASH_VARIANCE` (Cr over, Dr short); zero lines skipped, a negative movement swaps sides, nothing posted when nothing moved. Dated the Ulaanbaatar day of the close, described `«shift number — till · cashier»`.
- **The hand-over (ADR-0064 #3).** CLOSED → HANDED_OVER with an `OTHER_IN` voucher (`PaymentsService.recordInTx`) on the chosen cash box (default: the settings' box) against `POS_CASH`, for `counted − float kept` by default, `collectedBy` = the cashier, reference = the shift's number; once per shift (409 `POS_SHIFT_STATE_INVALID`).
- **Lock order.** The shift row (FOR UPDATE) → a return's sale and its lines (FOR UPDATE) → a credit part's customer row (FOR NO KEY UPDATE) and its Finance Core party (ADR-0109) → a sale's voucher codes and their batches by id, then the partners they bill (ADR-0110) → the SALESFLOOR `bin_stock` rows of the sold products in product order (FOR UPDATE) → inventory's posting locks (bins FOR SHARE, caches) → the number counters. Two sales of the same shift serialise on the shift; two tills run in parallel.
- **Numbers.** The edge allocates them per till and day (`posSaleNumber`, `posShiftNumber` in `@tugeel/shared/pos`); the centre stores them verbatim and refuses a repeat with `POS_NUMBER_TAKEN`. The test till allocates them on the centre with `NumberingService` (`POS_SALE:<node>:<till>` / `POS_SHIFT:<node>:<till>`, period = the day).
- **PINs.** Six digits; `PinHasher` = argon2id with `secret` = `POS_PIN_PEPPER` (else `AUTH_PEPPER`); five wrong tries lock the cashier for a minute (`verifyPin`); an admin unlocks. The edge gets the hashes in its snapshot and checks them offline with the same pepper (§9).
- **The edge token.** `tgs_<node id>_<32-byte base64url secret>`; the centre keeps the sha-256 of the whole token and its first 8 hex as the prefix shown in the admin; a new token replaces the old one at once. `EdgeTokenGuard` on `@Public()` routes, `@CurrentEdge()` for the node.
- **Voids (ADR-0104).** A replayed `VOID` needs its till active, its shift known (any state) or null, the cashier active, the approver an existing user, and `total = Σ qty × unit price` (`POS_TOTALS_MISMATCH` otherwise); it moves neither stock nor money. The approver's `pos.till.supervise` is checked on the edge, not again here (as for returns).
- **Receipt facts (ADR-0065).** A replayed SALE carries its receipt's state (`PENDING` with the PosAPI provider, NONE with the fake one); each later change is a `RECEIPT_STATUS` document with the sale's (or the return's) client id: `ebarimt_status`, `ddtd`, `posapi_date` verbatim, `inactive_id` (the receipt it replaced), never `bill_id_suffix`, a lottery or a QR. It is audited (`RECEIPT_STATUS`) when the state or the ДДТД changes. A receipt replaced by a partial return or cancelled by a full one is CANCELLED; a full return has no receipt of its own (NONE).
- **Settings.** `pos_settings` is one row with defaults until saved; the STORE warehouse must be of kind STORE, the hand-over box an active CASH account. The edge's own warehouse is on its row (the settings' warehouse is the admin's default).

- **Credit sales (ADR-0109).** A SALE with a CREDIT tender (at most one) names `customerId`; it is B2C with no TIN and e-Barimt NONE. The centre never refuses it for credit: it runs `PosCustomersService.decideInTx` (the wholesale rule: the customer's net receivable + its confirmed orders not yet invoiced + the credit part; HOLD, LIMIT, or INACTIVE for an inactive customer) and keeps a refusal as `credit_excess` (the excess past the limit, the whole credit on a hold or an inactive customer) for «Шалгасан» (`POST /pos/sales/:id/credit-review`, `pos.manage`). Finance's `PosCreditService.invoiceSaleInTx` writes the invoice in the same transaction: one `invoices` row for the sale (Н- number, `order_number` = the till's number, `payment_method` CREDIT, due on the settlement day), its lines with the VAT, posted as `BTO.SALES_INVOICE` — Dr AR for the credit part (the open item), Dr POS_CASH / CARD_CLEARING for what the till took, Cr POS_SALES net, Cr VAT_PAYABLE. A RETURN of it is refunded off the debt first (`refundSplit`, checked here): its CREDIT part makes a money-only credit note (`creditReturnInTx`, `BTO.SALES_RETURN`: Dr SALES_RETURN and VAT, Cr AR settling the invoice, Cr POS_CASH for the cash paid back); a return with no credit left is an ordinary cash return.
- **Coupons and partner vouchers (ADR-0110).** A sale names its codes in `vouchers` (id, code, kind, face, amount). A coupon's amount is what its code took off the lines (the `applied` entries with its `code`); each partner voucher has one VOUCHER tender (`voucherCode`) for its amount; no CREDIT beside a partner voucher; a return takes none. The codes and batches are locked (by id) after the customer, then the partners they bill; a code already REDEEMED, a stopped batch or a sold day outside the dates is kept as `pos_sale_vouchers.issue` (counted as `vouchersToCheck`, «Шалгасан» by `pos.manage`); an unknown code or a face value that is not its batch's is refused for good. The first use marks the code REDEEMED. Per sale and partner, finance's `PosVoucherBillingService.chargeInTx` writes a lineless invoice for the face value of the issue-free codes, due on the partner's settlement day, posted as `BTO.SALES_INVOICE`: Dr AR, Cr VOUCHER_CLEARING (what was used), Cr VOUCHER_BREAKAGE (the rest). A return of a sale with vouchers gives back at most its cash and card less earlier returns (409 `POS_RETURN_VOUCHER_PART`, `refundable`). Codes: `POS_VOUCHER_INVALID` (422, `reason`), `POS_VOUCHER_USED` (409), `POS_VOUCHER_WITH_CREDIT` (422), `POS_RETURN_VOUCHER_PART` (409), `POS_VOUCHER_NOTHING_TO_CHECK` (409).
- **The close with credit (ADR-0109).** `PosShiftsService.booked` sums what the invoiced sales and credited returns booked themselves (their totals and VAT, the cash and card the till took or paid back for them); `shiftCloseLines` leaves it out of POS_SALES, SALES_RETURN, VAT, POS_CASH and CARD_CLEARING. The counted and expected cash and the variance stay the drawer's; `credit_sales` / `credit_refunds` keep the CREDIT tenders for the Z. ADR-0110: Dr VOUCHER_CLEARING for the vouchers' tenders (`voucher_sales`); the partners' invoices empty it.

- **Payment types (ADR-0111).** A replayed tender names its `paymentTypeId` (null from older edges): the type must exist and have the tender's kind (an INACTIVE one still counts); the name kept is the till's. TRANSFER tenders are booked at the close on `TRANSFER_CLEARING` (account 120302, `TODO-VERIFY` with the accountant) and kept as `pos_shifts.transfer_sales` / `transfer_refunds`; a credit sale's transfer part is on its invoice. `GET /pos/shifts/:id` lists `payments` by type. A `DAY_COUNT` document is checked (notes of `DENOMINATIONS`, each once, the total their sum, the till active) and kept in `pos_day_counts`.

- **The bonus card (ADR-0112).** A sale or a return of a member carries `loyalty` (memberId, earnBase, percent, earned, spent, givenBack, clawback, spendDelta). `PosLoyaltyService.checkShape`: a sale earns `pointsEarned(loyaltyEarnBase(tenders), percent)` (floor; cash, card and transfer amounts), spends its one whole POINTS tender, never beside CREDIT, and adds its base to the lifetime; a return earns and spends nothing and its POINTS tender is the points given back. The member is locked after the codes, before the shelf; a return must equal `loyaltyReturn` from the sale's row and its earlier returns. A BLOCKED card or a spend past the centre's balance is kept with its `issue` (`loyaltyToCheck` in `/pos/attention`); an unknown member is rejected. The shift's close adds Dr LOYALTY_LIABILITY (320301) for points spent, Cr for points given back, Dr LOYALTY_EXPENSE (730301) / Cr the liability for points earned, the reverse for points taken back; an adjustment posts its own MANUAL journal entry (TODO-VERIFY D16). The tier is the edge's choice (the spend before the sale); the centre does not check it.

## 9. The store edge (S2, ADR-0067)

The same image as `ROLE=edge` (`apps/api/src/edge.ts`, `apps/api/src/edge/**`): no `modules/**`, only `common/**` and `@tugeel/shared`; the shapes it exchanges with the centre and the kiosk are in `@tugeel/shared/pos` (`edge.ts`).

- **Environment** (`EdgeEnvSchema`; `apps/api/.env.edge` locally, `deploy/edge/.env` in Docker): `EDGE_PORT` 3400, `EDGE_DATABASE_URL`, `EDGE_DB_POOL_MAX` 5, `CENTRAL_API_URL`, `EDGE_NODE_TOKEN`, `EDGE_JWT_SECRET` (no `dev-` value in production), `POS_PIN_PEPPER` (the centre's value, or its `AUTH_PEPPER`), `EBARIMT_PROVIDER` (`fake` or `posapi`, ADR-0065), `POSAPI_URL` and `EBARIMT_BRANCH_NO` (the merchant's branch number at PosAPI, digits; both required for posapi), `POSAPI_TIMEOUT_MS` (20 000), `EBARIMT_LOOKUP_URL` (`https://api.ebarimt.mn`), `EDGE_TIMERS`, `EDGE_PUSH_INTERVAL_MS`.
- **Tables** (its own database; `apps/api/src/edge/db`, migration `0000_edge`, applied by `db:migrate:edge` and by the entrypoint): `edge_state` (one row: the snapshot document and version, the pull, push and contact times, the last error), `edge_devices` (bound browsers: till, sha-256 of the device token, user agent, seen, revoked), `edge_sequences` (`SALE:<till>` / `SHIFT:<till>` per day), `edge_shifts` (one OPEN per till), `edge_sales` (the document and the receipt as sent), `edge_outbox` (`UNIQUE (kind, client_id)`; PENDING, then SENT or REJECTED), `edge_pin_attempts`; migration `0001_till` (ADR-0104): `edge_sales.kind` (SALE / RETURN) and `refunds_client_id`, `edge_voids`, `edge_parked`; migration `0002_ebarimt` (ADR-0065): `edge_receipts` (the PosAPI operations in order: ISSUE / CANCEL, the owner, chain and target sale, PENDING / SENDING / DONE / ERROR / INDETERMINATE / ABANDONED, the lines and payments to build from, the ДДТД, `posapi_date` verbatim, `inactive_id`, the message, attempts, the next try, `late`, who resolved it — never a lottery or a QR) and the PosAPI facts on `edge_state`; migration `0004_vouchers` (ADR-0110): `edge_state.vouchers` (the feed), `edge_voucher_uses` (one row per code a sale took, the code as key), `edge_sales.voucher_amount`.
- **Routes** (`/edge/v1`, `KioskGuard`: public, device, or device and cashier session): `GET health` and `POST pair` (public); `GET device` and `POST auth/pin` (device); `POST auth/logout`, `GET session`, `POST shifts/open`, `POST shifts/close`, `GET products/search?q=`, `GET products/barcode/:code`, `POST quote`, `POST sales`, `GET sales/recent`; ADR-0104: `GET quick-buttons`, `GET supervisors`, `POST voids`, `GET parked`, `POST parked`, `POST parked/:clientId/resume`, `POST parked/:clientId/discard`, `GET returns/sale?number=`, `POST returns` (cashier); ADR-0065: `POST sales` and `POST returns` answer `{ receipt, print, copy }`, `GET receipts/:clientId/print`, `GET ebarimt/taxpayer?no=`, `GET ebarimt/queue`, `POST ebarimt/:id/resolve` and `POST ebarimt/send-data` (a supervisor's approval). The device token travels in `x-bto-device`, the session as a bearer token.
- **Codes.** `POS_PAIRING_INVALID` (422), `POS_DEVICE_UNKNOWN` (401), `POS_PIN_INVALID` (401, `attemptsLeft`), `POS_CASHIER_LOCKED` (423, `until`), `POS_SESSION_EXPIRED` (401), `POS_SNAPSHOT_MISSING` (503: the edge has never reached the centre), `POS_SUPERVISOR_REQUIRED` (403: no approval while the store asks for one, or the approver is no supervisor), `POS_PARKED_LIMIT` (409, `max`), `EBARIMT_LOOKUP_UNAVAILABLE` (503), `EBARIMT_TAXPAYER_NOT_FOUND` (404), `EBARIMT_RECEIPT_STATE_INVALID` (409, `status`), `EBARIMT_POSAPI_UNAVAILABLE` (503), plus `POS_SHIFT_STATE_INVALID`, `POS_TOTALS_MISMATCH`, `POS_TILL_INACTIVE`, `POS_CASHIER_INACTIVE`, `PRICE_MISSING`.
- **Loops** (`SyncRunner`, off with `EDGE_TIMERS=false`): pull every `snapshot_interval_sec` (60), push every `EDGE_PUSH_INTERVAL_MS` while documents wait (back-off from 2 s to 60 s after a failure), heartbeat every 30 s (with the PosAPI facts); with `posapi` also `posapi-info` (`GET /rest/info` every 5 minutes) and `ebarimt` (due receipts every 10 s, `EbarimtService.runDue`). The tests call `pullOnce`, `pushOnce`, `heartbeatOnce` and `runDue`.
- **Rules.** A pairing code is checked against the till's hash in the snapshot (sha-256 of `<till id>:<code>`, not expired); one the snapshot does not know yet makes the edge pull once more. A PIN is checked against the snapshot's hash; five wrong ones lock the cashier on this edge for a minute; a session lives 12 hours, bound to the device and the till, and ends when the snapshot no longer lists the cashier as active. A sale is priced from the snapshot (RETAIL), refused when a line has no price, paid by cash (with the amount handed over and the change) and card (typed by hand) that make the total, and written with its document and its outbox entry in one edge transaction; a repeated client id answers its receipt. With the fake provider the receipt is a test receipt (`ebarimt.mode = TEST`, no ДДТД).
- **The till's tools (ADR-0104).** The search puts a typed barcode, then an exact SKU first. An approval is a supervisor's id and PIN, checked like a sign-in (the same lock); `approvalRequired` in the session mirrors the store's `supervisorRequired`. A void prices its lines with today's quote and is written with its outbox entry in one transaction. A parked basket belongs to its till (≤ 20); resuming deletes it and answers its lines; cancelling it writes a CART_CANCELLED void. A return finds a SALE of this edge by number (`tillCodeLookKey`), locks it, refuses past the window or beyond what earlier returns left (`POS_RETURN_WINDOW`, `POS_RETURN_EXCEEDS`), copies the sold unit price, shares the line's discount by quantity (the rest takes the rest), takes VAT at today's rate and pays one CASH tender; the shift's Z counts returns apart and takes the cash refunds out of the expected cash.
- **e-Barimt (ADR-0065).** `EbarimtService`: the sale's or the return's transaction queues its `edge_receipts` operations; right after the commit one try while the cashier waits (`afterCommit`), then the `ebarimt` loop in id order, an operation waiting for the receipt it changes. Claimed (SENDING) before each call; found SENDING at boot, an ISSUE becomes INDETERMINATE and a CANCEL is sent again. `PosapiClient` (`node:http`, a fresh connection per call): no connection → NOT_SENT (sent again), `ERROR`/`PAYMENT` → ERROR (tried again with back-off for a day), anything after the connection without a full answer → INDETERMINATE (only a supervisor's «Гараагүй» queues it again; «Гарсан» records the ДДТД and date from ebarimt.mn). A return with nothing left cancels the receipt in force (`DELETE {id, date}`); one with goods left issues their receipt with `inactiveId` (the card part kept up to the new total, the rest cash). The request is `buildPosapiReceipt` (`@tugeel/shared/mn`): the merchant's TIN from `/rest/info` (one merchant), the store's district code, the classification code product → category → store default. The lottery and the QR live only in `PrintableCache` (process memory): the till's last receipt until its next sale or shift close, a late receipt until printed once or its till's shift closes. A company receipt needs `TaxpayerClient.lookup` (online, cached 10 minutes). The kiosk prints with `window.print()` (`docs/runbooks/pos-till-printing.md`).
- **Payment rows, history and the day (ADR-0111).** The session carries `paymentTypes` (the snapshot's active types, in order). A sale's tenders name their type; the edge takes the kind from it (a tender with a kind only gets the first active type of it); cash rows are one CASH tender with `tendered`; TRANSFER reaches PosAPI as `BANK_TRANSFER`, and a partial return's replacement keeps the card and transfer parts. `GET sales?day=&q=` lists the till's sales and returns of a day (`edge_sales`); `GET day-summary?day=` sums the day by payment type (`paymentTotalsOf`, the till's order) with the first shift's opening and the expected cash; `POST day-summary` keeps the cashier's banknote count in `edge_day_counts` (edge migration `0005_day_counts`) and queues a `DAY_COUNT` document once per client id. The shift summary adds `transferSales` and `payments`. The kiosk prints the day slip (the payment lines with «/ ……», the non-empty notes, the two signatures) and a test slip («Хэвлэгч шалгах») through the browser's default printer.
- **The payment step (ADR-0109).** The kiosk sends the cashier's choices: `receiptType` («Хувь хүн» B2C, «Байгууллага» B2B, which needs `buyer`: 422 `POS_BUYER_REQUIRED`; an older kiosk without it gets B2B when a buyer is given), the tenders (CASH, CARD and at most one CREDIT), `customerId` with a CREDIT part (422 `POS_CUSTOMER_REQUIRED`) and `acceptOverLimit`. A sale with credit queues no receipt (e-Barimt NONE), is B2C and its receipt carries `credit` (customer, amount): the kiosk prints the credit slip twice («Харилцагчийн хувь», «Дэлгүүрийн хувь», a signature line, no VAT line, QR or lottery). The customer feed (`CustomerStore`, `edge_state.customers`, edge migration `0003_credit`) is pulled with the snapshot (`SyncRunner.pullCustomersOnce`); `GET customers?q=` and `GET customers/:id` (cashier) search it by code, name, phone, TIN or registration number with what is left of each limit. `CreditGate` before the sale's transaction: the centre's `credit-check` within `EDGE_CREDIT_CHECK_TIMEOUT_MS` (3 000) with this edge's credit not replayed yet (409 `CREDIT_BLOCKED` with `reason`, no tolerance); without an answer the feed: a hold refused, a passed limit 409 `POS_CREDIT_OFFLINE_OVER_LIMIT` (`available`, `excess`) until the cashier sends `acceptOverLimit`. A return of a credit sale (`edge_sales.credit_amount`, `customer_id`) is refunded off the debt first, the rest in cash, without e-Barimt; the found sale shows `credit` (customer, credit left). The shift's summary adds `creditSales` / `creditRefunds`.
- **Coupons and partner vouchers (ADR-0110).** `VoucherStore` keeps the centre's voucher feed (`SyncRunner.pullVouchersOnce`, after the customers). `GET vouchers/:code` (cashier) answers a code or 422 `POS_VOUCHER_INVALID` (`reason` UNKNOWN, USED, NOT_YET, EXPIRED, STOPPED, NOTHING_DUE); `POST quote` takes `codes` (coupons through the resolver's coupon stage, then the partner vouchers paying the total in order: `vouchers`, `voucherTotal`, `due`); `POST sales` takes `codes` and `voucherApproval` (always required with codes, 403 `POS_SUPERVISOR_REQUIRED`), derives the VOUCHER tenders, refuses credit beside a voucher (422 `POS_VOUCHER_WITH_CREDIT`), allows no tender when nothing is due, and inserts the uses in the sale's transaction (409 `POS_VOUCHER_USED`). `POST approvals/check` (cashier) checks a supervisor's PIN before the payment. The receipt's payments send the vouchers' part under `VOUCHER_PAYMENT_CODE` (CASH, TODO-VERIFY). A return of a sale with vouchers gives back at most its cash and card (`refundable` on the found sale; 409 `POS_RETURN_VOUCHER_PART`). The shift's summary adds `voucherSales`. The kiosk: «Купон, ваучер (F2)» or a scanned code opens the voucher dialog; «Төлбөр рүү» asks the PIN once and opens the payment for what is left.
- **The bonus card (ADR-0112).** `LoyaltyStore` keeps the centre's loyalty feed (`SyncRunner.pullLoyaltyOnce`, every pull) in `edge_state.loyalty` (edge migration `0006_loyalty`), indexed by phone; a member's balance and lifetime are the feed's plus this edge's own documents not yet counted (`edge_sales.points_delta` / `spend_delta`, not replayed or replayed after the feed was built). `GET members?phone=` answers `EdgeMember` (masked phone, tier, next tier) or `POS_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`; `POST members/:id/pin-check` checks the member's own PIN (argon2 with `POS_PIN_PEPPER`), five wrong ones lock the card for 15 minutes (`edge_member_pin_attempts`, reset by a new `pinVersion`). `POST sales` with `member` refuses a blocked card (`POS_MEMBER_BLOCKED`) and credit (`POS_POINTS_WITH_CREDIT`), checks the PIN again when a whole POINTS tender spends points, takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the member inside the sale and refuses a spend past the balance (`POS_POINTS_INSUFFICIENT`), earns at the tier of the spend before the sale, and prints the loyalty block. A return of a member sale gives the points back first (a POINTS tender), then cash, and takes the earned points back by share (`loyaltyReturn`). The points part goes to PosAPI under `LOYALTY_PAYMENT_CODE` (CASH, TODO-VERIFY). The kiosk: «Бонус карт (F3)» (phone → member → «Холбох»), the payment's «Бонус оноо» row with the member's PIN and «Олох оноо ≈», CREDIT hidden beside a member.
- **Running it.** Docker (the owner's PC now, the store PC in S5): `pnpm edge:up`, `pnpm edge:down`, `pnpm edge:logs` over `deploy/edge/docker-compose.edge.yml` (`edge-db`, `edge`, `kiosk` on `KIOSK_PORT` 7400); the tills open `http://<LAN address>:7400`. Without Docker: `pnpm --filter @tugeel/api db:migrate:edge`, then `pnpm --filter @tugeel/api edge:dev` (port 3400, `apps/api/.env.edge`), and the kiosk at `http://localhost:5173/pos.html` (Vite proxies `/edge`).
- **First use.** In the admin: the store's settings, an edge server with its token (into the edge's env), a till, the cashiers' PINs; then «Холбох код» on the till, typed once into the till's browser with the till's code.
