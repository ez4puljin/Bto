# Module `inventory` — contract

Status: **built for delivery 1**: core in week 3, two-step transfers and the bulk services of the imports in week 4 (both 2026-10-11), the movement history, the stocktake REST and screens, the low-stock list and the nightly jobs below the cut line (2026-10-13); accepted locally together with the rest of delivery 1. Product registration (ADR-0044 #5) runs here since 2026-10-13. Code: `apps/api/src/modules/inventory/`, shared rules in `packages/shared/src/inventory`, screens in `apps/web/src/features/inventory/`. Decisions: ADR-0007 (ledger and caches), ADR-0016, ADR-0019 (lots, reservations), ADR-0027 (access control), ADR-0033 (receipts and costing), ADR-0034 (posting protocol), ADR-0035 (transfers), ADR-0036 (opening stock through imports), ADR-0038 (blind stocktakes), ADR-0039 (low-stock list), ADR-0040 (nightly jobs), architecture §4 (inventory row), §5.1 and §5.3.

inventory depends on `identity` (audit, numbering, scopes, warehouses, `WAREHOUSE_CREATED_HOOK`), `catalog` (products, unit conversion, barcodes, the catalog part of a registration) and `partners` (active suppliers, supplier links). imports, sales, delivery, procurement, POS and reporting import its `index.ts`.

## 1. Owner's answers behind the design (interview of 2026-10-11)

- **Warehouses and shelves.** Four or more wholesale warehouses besides the supermarket; numbered shelves, 300 and more per warehouse, each with a barcode label whose text is the shelf code (`A-01-03`: row letter, rack, level). Labels exist, so the system does not print them.
- **Home warehouse.** The Excel column "харьяалагдах агуулах" means a product is stored only there; receiving it into another wholesale warehouse is refused. A product usually sits on one fixed shelf.
- **Receiving.** Warehouse staff count the goods and register them; they do not know prices and must not see costs. The goods go straight onto the shelf and are sold at once. The accountant enters the supplier's prices ex-VAT separately, the same day; mistakes are corrected until the end of the next month. Suppliers add free bonus goods.
- **Adjustments.** Reasons: damaged, expired, shortage (lost), surplus (found). Warehouse staff create them, an admin approves.
- **Stocktakes.** Done when there is time, on no fixed day. Blind (2026-10-13, ADR-0038): counters see only what they counted themselves, not the expected products, quantities or differences; only the approver (the admin) sees those. The stock pages stay readable by everyone, counted shelves included.
- **Low stock (2026-10-13, ADR-0039).** The list shows products at or below their minimum and products a warehouse held that ran out, even without a minimum, with the quantity up to the maximum; levels come from the product page and an Excel template; the supermarket turns rows into a transfer request, a wholesale list shows the main supplier.
- **Devices.** Computer + USB scanner for now; no phone camera, so no LAN HTTPS during development.
- **Transfers (week 4 interview).** Goods move from the wholesale warehouses to the supermarket, between wholesale warehouses and back from the supermarket. The receiving side asks and the sender fulfils; the supermarket sends its returns on its own. The receiver counts in the unit that was shipped (the supermarket's sales clerks at the store) and the supermarket puts goods straight onto the sales floor. A shortfall goes back to the sender (who miscounted), a surplus comes out of the sender's stock. A product with a home wholesale warehouse is never sent to another wholesale warehouse. One shelf per product per transfer; the list travels on paper.

## 2. Owned tables

Migration `0005_inventory` (generated, then CHECKs, triggers and REVOKEs by hand), `0006_inventory_ledger` (`--custom`, hand-written; the Drizzle objects live in `ledger.schema.ts`, which the aggregator does not export, ADR-0034 #6) and `0007_inventory_transfers` (generated, then the `stock_ledger_leg` CHECK replaced and the REVOKE by hand); `0009_stock_ledger_indexes` (`--custom`) and `0010_stocktake_counting` (generated).

| Table | Purpose | Key rules (DB-enforced unless noted) |
|---|---|---|
| `bins` | shelves and system bins | `code` 1–32 `^[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9._/-]*$`, unique per warehouse (`bins_warehouse_code_key`); `type` one of the ten §5.1 types, `sellable` generated (STORAGE, PICKING, SALESFLOOR); `is_system` = not STORAGE/PICKING, one system bin per warehouse and type (`bins_one_system_type`); `status` ACTIVE/COUNTING with `locked_by_stocktake_id` exactly while COUNTING; `archived_at` never while COUNTING; `(warehouse_id, id)` unique as the target of composite FKs; trigger `bins_fixed_columns` keeps warehouse, code, type and the system flag (23001) |
| `product_locations` | home warehouse, fixed shelf, levels | `(warehouse_id, product_id)` unique; one `is_home` per product (`product_locations_one_home`); `default_bin_id` composite FK to a bin of the same warehouse; `min_qty ≤ max_qty` |
| `lots` | lots (empty until ADR-0019 is activated) | `(product_id, lot_no)` unique |
| `warehouse_stock` | balance per warehouse, product, lot | `UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT` key; `on_hand ≥ 0`, `reserved ≥ 0`, `0 ≤ sellable_on_hand ≤ on_hand`; `avg_cost NUMERIC(14,4)`; fillfactor 80; written only by `StockPostingService` |
| `bin_stock` | balance per bin, product, lot | `UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT` key; composite FK to its bin; `on_hand ≥ 0`; written only by `StockPostingService` |
| `stock_reservations` | warehouse-level reservations of document lines (written by `ReservationService` since 2026-10-15, ADR-0055) | one active row per document line (`NULLS NOT DISTINCT` partial unique) |
| `goods_receipts` + `goods_receipt_lines` | `GR-` | DRAFT/POSTED/CANCELLED; supplier, delivery note number and date; `costing_status` PENDING/COSTED, `costing_version`, `costed_at`; POSTED ⇔ number, `posted_at`, `effective_date`; lines: unit, entered qty, `factor_snapshot`, `qty_base`, bin, lot |
| `bin_moves` + `bin_move_lines` | `BM-` | from ≠ to bin; posted on creation |
| `adjustments` + `adjustment_lines` | `ADJ-` | `reason` DAMAGED/EXPIRED/SHORTAGE/SURPLUS/OPENING/OPENING_REVERSAL/POS_SHORTFALL (the last two only from the system: `reverseOpeningInTx`, and the pos module’s `postSystemInTx` when a till sold what the shelf lacked, ADR-0063 #5); DRAFT/SUBMITTED/POSTED/CANCELLED with `submitted_at`/`submitted_by`; lines carry an optional `unit_cost` (inbound reasons); `reverses_adjustment_id` unique (an opening is reversed at most once) |
| `transfers` + `transfer_lines` (0007) | `TR-` (ADR-0035) | `origin` REQUEST/PUSH; DRAFT/REQUESTED/SHIPPED/RECEIVED/CANCELLED with the columns of each state CHECKed (number from REQUESTED and kept by a REQUEST draft taken back for editing, 0018 / ADR-0046; `shipped_at/by` + `ship_date`, `received_at/by` + `receive_date`, `cancelled_at/by` + `cancel_reason`); source ≠ destination; indexes (source, status, id) and (destination, status, id); lines: product, request unit + frozen `request_factor`, `requested_qty`, `shipped_qty_base` + `from_bin_id`, `received_qty_base` + `to_bin_id` (received needs shipped), lot; `(transfer, product, lot)` unique NULLS NOT DISTINCT |
| `stocktakes` + `stocktake_bins` + `stocktake_lines` | `ST-` (ADR-0038) | DRAFT/COUNTING/REVIEW/APPROVED/CANCELLED; `started_at` exactly from COUNTING on; bins: `finished_at`/`finished_by` together (0010); lines: frozen `expected_qty`, `counted_qty ≥ 0`, `counted_at` (a person counted it; NULL with a count = not found, needs a count: `stocktake_lines_counted`, 0010), surplus `unit_cost`; `(stocktake, bin, product, lot)` unique NULLS NOT DISTINCT; bins and lines may be deleted by the service (a recount drops found lines) |
| `reconciliation_runs` | every check of the caches | OK/DRIFT, `diff_count` (0 ⇔ OK), the first 100 differences |
| `stock_ledger` (0006) | the append-only movement ledger | movement type, sign and leg checked per type (`TRANSFER_OUT` legs 1, 3, 5, `TRANSFER_IN` 2, 4, 6 since 0007); `abs(qty_base) = entered_qty × factor_snapshot`; `unit_cost ≥ 0`; composite FK to the bin; idempotency key `stock_ledger_idem` (doc type, line, movement, leg); indexes (product, id), (doc type, doc id), BRIN `posted_at`, and since `0009_stock_ledger_indexes` (bin, id) and (warehouse, id) for the movement history; `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE` + triggers `stock_ledger_no_update_delete` / `_no_truncate` (23001 even for the owner) |
| `stock_valuation_adjustments` (0006) | costing history of receipt lines (ADR-0033 #5) | one row per line and version; `value_delta = amount − value_before`, `absorbed + variance = value_delta`, free goods ⇒ amount 0; the ledger's guards |
| `stock_snapshot_daily`, `stock_snapshot_bin_monthly` (0006) | snapshots (D9) | partitioned by month: 0006 created 2026-10 … 2028-09, the monthly job keeps this month to 24 months ahead and drops months more than 13 back through `maintain_stock_snapshot_partitions()` (0012: SECURITY DEFINER owned by `migrator`, fixed `search_path`, EXECUTE for `app` only); `REVOKE DELETE, TRUNCATE` on the tables and every partition |

Document headers (`goods_receipts`, `bin_moves`, `adjustments`, `stocktakes`, `transfers`) and the caches are `REVOKE DELETE, TRUNCATE … FROM app`; draft lines are deleted by their service only. The warehouse's «Тавиургүй» shelf (`NOSHELF`) is an ordinary STORAGE bin the opening-stock import creates on demand (sellable).

External documents on the ledger (`external: true`, numbered by their own module): `SO` and `PK` from sales, `CN` from finance (credit notes, `RETURN_IN` into the RETURNS bin by default; ADR-0060 #6), `POS` from the pos module (a till’s sale: `POS_SALE` from SALESFLOOR; a return: `RETURN_IN` onto SALESFLOOR or DAMAGED; ADR-0063). `movements()` reads their numbers from `sales_orders.number`, `credit_notes.number` and `pos_sales.number`.

## 5. Rules worth knowing

- **Posting protocol (ADR-0034).** Lock order: document headers (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE` and a status check; the final UPDATE sets status, number and dates) → lines → bins `FOR SHARE` in id order (COUNTING → `BIN_LOCKED_FOR_COUNT`, archived → `BIN_ARCHIVED`) → missing cache rows of inbound keys (one sorted `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) → `warehouse_stock` `FOR UPDATE` → `bin_stock` `FOR UPDATE` → ledger → cache UPDATEs → numbers last. Keys are matched through `VALUES … IS NOT DISTINCT FROM`, so NULL lots lock correctly. Deltas are summed per key; any bin below zero is `STOCK_INSUFFICIENT` with `binCode`, `available` and `requested`. One `now()` per posting gives `posted_at`, the Ulaanbaatar `effective_date` and the number's year.
- **Costs (ADR-0007, ADR-0033).** Outbound legs carry the average; inbound legs the explicit cost, else the paired outbound leg's, else the average. A receipt posts at the average and the accountant's costing applies the coverage rule (absorbed = Δ × min(Q, on hand) / Q); the rest is a recorded variance. Adjustments and count surpluses take the given cost or the average.
- **Table order of product writers (ADR-0044 #5).** A registration, the product import and its revert take products (by id; the import locks the existing ones first), then `product_locations`, then `product_suppliers`. The revert once took `product_suppliers` first and could deadlock with a registration; `registration-races` keeps the order.
- **Home warehouse.** Receipts, SURPLUS/OPENING adjustments and transfer lines (added or shipped) into a WHOLESALE warehouse other than the product's home are refused (`PRODUCT_NOT_IN_WAREHOUSE`, `params.warehouse` = the home's name); write-offs, moves, count corrections and transfer receipts never are (shipped goods never stay stuck in TRANSIT).
- **Transfers (ADR-0035).** Only WHOLESALE and STORE warehouses. REQUEST: DRAFT → REQUESTED → SHIPPED → RECEIVED; PUSH: DRAFT → SHIPPED → RECEIVED; DRAFT and REQUESTED can be cancelled, SHIPPED never (receiving nothing returns everything); the destination may take a REQUESTED transfer back to DRAFT to edit it (ADR-0046): it keeps its number and is sent again under it, and while it is a draft the source can neither ship nor decline it (the header lock makes exactly one of a shipment and a take-back win). Scopes: the side that created a draft edits it; the destination requests, withdraws and receives; the source ships, declines and pushes. The `TR-` number comes with the request (a push: at shipping); a cancelled request keeps it. Every leg is in base units: ship = leg 1 out of the source shelf (scanned; default the fixed shelf, SALESFLOOR at the supermarket) and leg 2 into TRANSIT; receive with s shipped and r received = leg 3 out of TRANSIT (−s, at the cost leg 2 put in, so TRANSIT returns to zero per line in quantity and value), leg 4 into the destination (+min(r, s): the supermarket's SALESFLOOR, a wholesale fixed shelf, else RECEIVING; a scanned bin overrides), a shortfall leg 6 back into the source bin (its RECEIVING if that shelf was archived), a surplus leg 5 out of the source bin (else the source shelf holding the most, else `STOCK_INSUFFICIENT`) and leg 6 into the destination. Counts are entered in the line's unit or another current unit and must be exact in base units. At most 3,000 lines, so a receive with a surplus on every line (4 legs) fits one posting.
- **Stocktakes (ADR-0038).** Starting locks the bins `FOR UPDATE` in id order, refuses bins another count holds, sets them COUNTING and freezes `bin_stock` (read after the locks, one `INSERT … SELECT`, at most 10,000 lines). Counting is blind: counters receive only lines with `counted_at`, never frozen quantities, differences or line totals. A scan adds `COALESCE(counted, 0) + qty × factor` in one statement under the header lock (concurrent scans all count; the `Idempotency-Key` makes a retried scan count once); a take-back is an UPDATE because PostgreSQL checks an upsert's proposed row before its conflict. Typed counts replace the last (with `previousQty` as a guard). Finishing a shelf sets its uncounted lines to 0 without `counted_at` (not found) and marks it; scans may still add to it. Review needs every shelf finished; a recount clears the chosen shelves and returns to COUNTING with the shelves still locked and frozen. Approval checks that the frozen quantities still match `bin_stock` (409 `CONFLICT` otherwise), frees the bins and posts COUNT_CORRECTION = counted − frozen in the base unit; a count without differences still gets its `ST-` number. Cancelling frees the bins without postings; in review only an approver cancels.
- **Low stock (ADR-0039).** Levels live in `product_locations` per warehouse (base units, min ≤ max, both ≥ 0); the product page sets them one warehouse at a time (the home rule applies), the `LEVELS` import in bulk. The list reads `product_locations` and `warehouse_stock` only: a product a warehouse once held stays `OUT` until its balance moves. Available stock replaces on-hand once reservations exist (delivery 2).
- **Numbers.** `GR-`, `BM-`, `ADJ-`, `ST-` + year + 6 digits, allocated at posting inside its transaction; a refused posting uses no number. `TR-` is allocated by the transfer itself (request or push shipping).
- **Bulk writes for imports (ADR-0036).** Methods called with many products in one transaction (`setManyInTx`, `postSystemInTx`) take table locks or chunked statements instead of per-product advisory locks, so a 10,000-product import stays within PostgreSQL's lock table.
- **Scanning (web).** Scan contexts read Latin letters and `-` `.` `/` by physical key, so labels scan on the Mongolian layout; a scan that lands in a quantity field goes to the scan box and the quantity is restored.
- **Advisory locks.** `72_028_001` system bins, `72_028_002` product home (per product).

## 7. Commands

- `pnpm inventory:reconcile` — compares both caches with the ledger in one REPEATABLE READ snapshot, writes the day's warehouse snapshot and a `reconciliation_runs` row; prints the first 20 differences and exits with 2 on drift.
- Jobs (worker, ADR-0040): `inventory.reconcile` every night at 03:00 Ulaanbaatar time (`reconcile('NIGHTLY')`; a drift is logged as an error and raises the banner, the job succeeds), `inventory.snapshotMonthly` at 03:30 on the 1st (partitions, then the bin snapshot). A night the worker was down is made up once when it starts; a whole missed month has no bin snapshot.
- `pnpm inventory:rebuild-balances` — recomputes `bin_stock` and `warehouse_stock` quantities from the ledger with both tables locked against postings (averages are kept; a missing warehouse row takes the latest known average), then reconciles. Find the cause of a drift before running it.

## 8. Audit

Actions `CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `ARCHIVE`, `POST`, `CANCEL`, `SUBMIT`, `RETURN`, `COST`, `START`, `REVIEW`, `REQUEST`, `SHIP`, `RECEIVE` on `bins` (a bulk creation is one row), `product_locations`, `goods_receipts`, `bin_moves`, `adjustments`, `stocktakes`, `transfers`. Draft line edits are not audited; the posting audit row carries the lines. Stocktakes: scans and typed counts are not audited (the lines keep `counted_at`); finishing a shelf is an `UPDATE` row (`binId`, how many lines were not found), a recount a `RETURN` row with the cleared counts. Bulk writes for imports record one row per entity through `AuditService.recordMany`.
