# Logos

The only drawn marks in the repository, copied from `apps/mobile/assets`. The web has no logo file: its mark is the live `BrandMark`.

| File | What | Ink and ground |
|---|---|---|
| `staff-icon.png` | The staff app's icon, 1024×1024 | White geometric «B» on `primary` |
| `staff-splash-icon.png` | The staff app's splash mark, 512×512, shown 96 wide on white | A rounded `primary` square with the white «B» |
| `staff-adaptive-icon.png` | Android's foreground layer, 1024×1024 | White on transparent: place it on `#0071e3`, it looks empty on white |
| `bto300-icon.png` | Bto300's icon, 1024×1024 | White «B300» in Inter on `primary` |
| `bto300-splash-icon.png` | Bto300's splash mark, 512×512, shown 96 wide on white | A rounded `primary` square with the white «B300» |
| `bto300-adaptive-icon.png` | Android's foreground layer, 1024×1024 | White on transparent: place it on `#0071e3`, it looks empty on white |

Use a mark at its own proportions on its own ground. Do not recolour, redraw or outline it. Text beside a mark is **Bto** or **Bto300**, never «Tugeel».
