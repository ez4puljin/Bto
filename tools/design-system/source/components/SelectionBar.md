A bar floating at the foot of a list while rows are ticked: how many, what can be done, «Сонголтыг цуцлах».

**Props:** `count`, `onClear`, `children` (the actions, outline and small). It shows nothing at zero.

**Rules:** each action opens a dialog naming the count and its rule. A change that may surprise runs a dry run first and names the records. A toast says what changed, what already was so and what was skipped, and the ticks clear. Ticks stay while the search and filters change.
