«Баганууд»: shows or hides each column of a list. Locked columns always show.

**When:** at the end of a `FilterBar`, on a list wider than 1366 px that keeps some columns out of its default set.

**Props:** `keys`, `locked`, `label(key)`, `hidden` (a set), `toggle(key)`, `showAll()`. The caller keeps the choice, per user, with `useHiddenColumns` in the app.
